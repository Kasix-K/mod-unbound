## 2026-10-15

### Added
- **Bulletin Board** (gameobject 920000, `tools/bulletin_board.py`, `src/unbound_bulletin_board.cpp`): a board in
  Stormwind's Trade District and Orgrimmar's Valley of Strength (both usable by both factions), level 60 only,
  gold rewards only (no attunement progress). **Dailies**: 2 kill bounties a day from 16 (level 55-60 creatures
  in the Plaguelands, Silithus, Winterspring and the Burning Steppes, 15 gold), and for every profession one
  gathering (Mining, Herbalism, Skinning, Fishing; 12 gold) or crafting quest (Alchemy, Blacksmithing,
  Leatherworking, Tailoring, Engineering, Enchanting, Cooking, First Aid; 15 gold) a day, shown only to players
  with that profession. **Weeklies**: kill the final boss of this week's dungeon and raid, one per tier, never
  the same dungeon (raid) for two tiers: Normal dungeon 50 gold, Heroic dungeon 100 gold, Normal raid 100 gold;
  Mythic dungeons and Heroic / Mythic raids are already picked each week and get their quests once those
  modes exist. GM commands `.bulletinboard show` and `.bulletinboard reroll`.
- **Raid Finder** (new **UnboundRaidFinder** addon, `src/unbound_raid_finder.cpp`): "Dungeons" and "Raids" tabs
  under the Dungeon Finder window (`/raidfinder` opens the Raids tab). It lists every raid by expansion with its
  level and whether you can enter it (available, level, attunement for classic Naxxramas, not released yet).
  No queue: solo you enter straight away; in a group only the leader picks, goes in first, and every other
  member gets a prompt ("<leader>'s group is entering <raid>. Enter the raid?", the game's summon prompt, two
  minutes) and is teleported next to the leader on accepting. Members who can't enter are told why; playerbots
  come along. "Leave Raid" sends you back to where you joined from (kept across relogs). Setting
  `Unbound.RaidFinder.Enable`.
- **Mythic maximum +10** (power budget): every Mythic dungeon and raid stops at +10, the number all Mythic
  systems are designed around (was a configurable +30). Mythic gear, upgrades and the climb estimates
  are reworked for it; Naxxramas Mythic +10 is the top of the game.
- **Soloable raid mechanics, Naxxramas: Thaddius** (`Unbound.SoloRaid.Thaddius`): alone in the raid, Stalagg
  and Feugen don't revive each other (normally both must die within 5 seconds, on platforms far apart) and
  don't overload their Tesla Coils when they follow the player off their platforms.
- **Endgame goals in the power budget:** classic Naxxramas Mythic +1 solo is the realistic endgame goal,
  Naxxramas Mythic +10 the stretch goal, with player power estimates per classic raid.
- **Soloable raid mechanics, Molten Core, Zul'Gurub and Ahn'Qiraj** (`src/unbound_solo_raids.cpp`), like Blackwing
  Lair: only while the raid has at most one player in it. Molten Core: Lucifron's Flamewaker Protectors'
  Dominate Mind fails. Zul'Gurub: Jin'do's Brain Wash Totem and Banish fail; Hakkar's Cause Insanity and Blood
  Siphon fail. Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj: Ayamiss' Paralyze and the larva's Feed fail. Temple of Ahn'Qiraj: Skeram's
  True Fulfillment, the Twin Emperors' Heal Brother and the Qiraji Mindslayers' Cause Insanity fail, and every
  hit on Viscidus counts 10 times towards freezing (200 frost hits) and shattering (150 melee hits) him
  (`Unbound.SoloRaid.ViscidusHitMultiplier`). Each boss can be switched off in `mod_unbound.conf`.
- **Classic Naxxramas** (40 players, level 60), taken from mod-individual-progression (ZhengPeiRu21; Naxx 40
  scripts and data by Sogladev; MIT license), only its Naxxramas parts (`src/naxx40`,
  `data/sql/db-world/unbound_naxx40*.sql`). The floating Necropolis is back above Plaguewood in the Eastern
  Plaguelands, with a "Teleport To Naxxramas" runestone and a meeting stone below it; the runestone is the only
  way in (the Stratholme back entrance is left out). It needs the classic attunement, "The Dread Citadel -
  Naxxramas" from Archmage Angela Dosantos at Light's Hope, and takes the group in on Naxxramas' 40-player
  difficulty (the level 80 10 and 25 player versions stay locked). All 15 bosses with their classic scripts,
  classic creatures and models, classic loot (Tier 3 and the rest), the Light's Hope Tier 3 quests, Master
  Craftsman Omarion, Archmage Tarsis Kir-Moldir, Frozen Runes and the Four Horsemen chest. Optional easier
  Four Horsemen, Gluth, Patchwerk and Razuvious (`Unbound.Naxx40.Doable.*`, off by default). Solo: the
  40-player raid scaling (about 2.8 players' worth). The expansion lock lets players into this version only.
- **Attunement tags regenerated** (`tools/item_expansions.py`): classic Onyxia and Naxxramas loot counts (195
  items, Naxxramas' creatures are classic although map 533 is Wrath), and so does the Heroic loot of batches 1-4
  (910122-910549), which the committed tag file was missing. Onyxia's level 80 loot and the Onyxia Scale Cloak
  and Breastplate no longer count (no source).
- **Onyxia's Lair is a classic level 60 raid again** (it was the level 80 version from 3.2): entry from level
  60 (10-player difficulty; the 25-player one stays level 80, so it can't be entered). Onyxia is level 63 with
  her classic health (1.1 million), armor and melee; Onyxian Whelps and Warders are classic too, and the
  Onyxian Lair Guards (added in 3.2) are gone. Her spells keep their classic ids but were raised to level 80
  damage in 3.2, so in the lair they deal their classic damage again (Flame Breath 3063-3937 instead of
  18850-21150; also Wing Buffet, Fireball, Tail Sweep, Deep Breath, Eruption, Heated Ground and the Warders'
  Fire Nova and Flame Lash; `src/unbound_classic_onyxia.cpp`). Classic loot: two Tier 2 helms, one of
  Sapphiron Drape / Ring of Binding / Eskhandar's Collar / Ancient Cornerstone Grimoire, 24% one of
  Deathbringer / Vis'kag / Shard of the Scale, the Onyxia Hide Backpack and the Head of Onyxia (level 80 gear,
  emblems and the Onyxian Drake mount removed). The classic Head of Onyxia quests are back (Thrall and
  Overlord Runthak; King Varian Wrynn instead of Bolvar and Major Mattingly), with the Rallying Cry of the
  Dragonslayer. Solo: about 2.8 players' worth like the other classic raids
  (`AutoBalance.InflectionPoint.PerInstance`). She can't be skinned: Scale of Onyxia and the Onyxia Scale
  Cloak stay unobtainable.
- **Soloable raid mechanics, Blackwing Lair** (`src/unbound_solo_raids.cpp`): mechanics no single player can
  handle, changed only while the raid has at most one player in it (GMs don't count; pets aren't players,
  playerbots are). With two or more players every fight is unchanged. Razorgore: Grethok the Controller's
  Dominate Mind fails, and the player breaks the eggs by walking over or clicking them (the Orb of Domination still
  works; killing Razorgore before the last egg still blows up the room). Vaelastrasz: Burning Adrenaline
  fails. Ebonroc: Shadow of Ebonroc fails. Chromaggus: Time Lapse and Chromatic Mutation fail. Nefarian:
  Lord Victor Nefarius' Shadow Command fails. Each boss can be switched off in `mod_unbound.conf`
  (`Unbound.SoloRaid.*`). Shadowflame still needs an Onyxia Scale Cloak (a custom way to get one is planned).
- **Ranks 4-5 for the Defensive and Utility perks**, the same way as the Offensive ones: about 1.3x / 1.5x
  of Rank 3 (e.g. Reinforced Armor 13 / 15%, Iron Will 35 / 40%, Vampirism 6.5 / 7.5%), GM-only for now
  (level 255). Thresholds, durations, delays and drawbacks stay the same; Steady Hands' pushback stops at
  90% and Juggernaut's cap goes down (27 / 25%, lower is stronger). All 57 Offensive, Defensive and Utility
  perks now have 5 ranks.
- **Offensive perk ranks.** All 25 Offensive perks have 5 ranks: Ranks 1-3 at levels 1 / 30 / 60 (Rank 3 is
  the value from before ranks existed, so level 60 characters lose nothing; lower levels get less), Ranks 4-5
  for the endgame (a +10% perk: 4 / 7 / 10 / 13 / 15%), the "Perk ranks" part of the power budget (about x1.3
  on top of perks). Ranks 4-5 unlock at level 255 for now, i.e. only with `.perk rank`, until a way to earn
  them is built. Only the upside is ranked; drawbacks and caps stay. All values in `mod_unbound.conf`. The
  perk window shows "3/5" badges and every rank in the tooltip ("endgame, coming later" for Ranks 4-5).

## 2026-10-14

### Added
- **Heroic Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul, Scholomance and Stratholme** (classic Heroic batch 4, the last): level
  60-62 with 1.5x health and damage; Blackrock Spire's Heroic is for up to 15 players like its normal mode.
  Includes the creatures their events summon (Gyth, the Rookery and Urok events, Kirtonos, Ramstein, Balnazzar,
  the Postmaster, the Brazier of Beckoning bosses). Heroic loot: 181 level 60 blue remakes of each boss's own
  drops (one per boss, two from each wing's last boss) plus level 60 gold; they count for attunement. All 19
  classic dungeons now have a Heroic mode.
- **Heroic Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, Sunken Temple and Blackrock Depths** (classic Heroic batch 3): level 60-62 with
  1.5x health and damage, including the creatures their events summon (Gahz'rilla, the pyramid event's
  Sandfury waves, Nekrum and Sezz'ziz, Noxxious Scions, the Avatar and Shade of Hakkar, the Ring of Law waves
  and bosses, Watchman Doomgrip). Heroic loot: 120 level 60 blue remakes of each boss's own drops (one per
  boss, two from the final boss) plus level 60 gold; they count for attunement.
- **Heroic Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery (all 4 wings), Razorfen Downs and Uldaman** (classic Heroic batch 2):
  level 60-62 with 1.5x health and damage, including the creatures their events summon (Tuten'kash, Plaguemaw
  the Rotting, Tomb Fiends and Reavers, Scarlet Scryers and Trainees). Heroic loot: 68 level 60 blue remakes of
  each boss's own drops (one per boss, two from the final boss and each Scarlet Monastery wing's last boss) plus
  level 60 gold; they count for attunement.
- **Heroic Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps and Gnomeregan** (classic Heroic batch 1): level
  60-62 with 1.5x health and damage like the others, including the creatures their events summon (Mutanus the
  Devourer, Baron Aquanis, Grubbis, Chomper, Arugal's Voidwalker, Walking Bombs, the Naralex and Aku'mai event
  adds). Heroic loot: 59 level 60 blue remakes of each boss's own drops (one per boss, two from the final boss)
  plus level 60 gold; they count for attunement.
- **12 more Defensive perks** (20 in all, still at most 3 active), 3 ranks each at levels 1 / 30 / 60:
  Juggernaut (no hit over 40/35/30% of maximum health, once every 3 s), Counterweight (next hit after a
  dodge, parry or block -10/15/20%), Titan's Resolve (up to 5/7.5/10% damage reduction as health drops),
  Second Wind (a hit over 15% of maximum health: heal 1/1.5/2% a second for 5 s), Hardened Flesh (the part
  of a hit over 10% of maximum health -10/15/20%), Pet's Blessing (pet damage taken -10/15/20%), Shield
  Wall Training (+0.5/0.75/1% block per block for 5 s, 5 stacks), Survival Instinct (below 30% health:
  damage taken -8/12/15%), Vital Reserve (maximum health +4/7/10%), Enduring (healing received +4/7/10%),
  Thornmail (10/20/30% of melee damage taken dealt back) and Heavy Frame (armor +5/10/15%). Most also work
  for your pet. New server-only spells 200214 (health and armor) and 200215 (a proc aura for dodges,
  parries, blocks and melee hits taken).
- **Gear attunement, stage 4 of 4: tooltips and the collection window** (new **UnboundAttunement** addon). Item
  tooltips show each weapon or armour piece's attunement: attuned, % and kills, sacrifice only (with the price),
  or why it doesn't count. The **Attunement** button in the micro menu (between Talents and Achievements) or `/attunement` opens the
  collection window: attuned items and items in progress as an icon grid, the stats they give, and a bar showing
  the diminishing returns. Tooltips and the window update live as you kill, attune and sacrifice. Gear attunement
  is complete.
- **World boss announcements** (`src/unbound_world_bosses.cpp`): the whole server is told 10 and 5 minutes
  before a world boss spawns, when it spawns (with the location), when it dies (with who killed it), and
  when nobody killed it within 24 hours, after which it despawns (never mid-fight) and its 3-day respawn
  timer starts again. The boss's grid is loaded just before it respawns and kept loaded while it lives, so
  this works with nobody nearby; the spawn time survives restarts. Hrimthar's yells are now zone-wide
  instead of world-wide. Settings: `Unbound.WorldBoss.Announce`, `.Warnings`, `.DespawnHours`.
- **Gear attunement, stage 3 of 4: the Altar of Sacrifice** (creature 190103, place it with `.npc add 190103`).
  Talking to it lists the items in your bags that count and aren't attuned yet; picking one shows the game's
  confirmation box with the gold price (2 / 10 / 50 / 250 for green / blue / epic / legendary, double for gear you
  could attune by wearing it). The item is destroyed and attuned at once, at full value.
- **8 Defensive perks**, all with 3 ranks (levels 1 / 30 / 60, values in `mod_unbound.conf`): Greater
  Bulwark, Evasive Maneuvers and Parry King (all your other two melee avoidance chances become Block, Dodge
  or Parry at 100/110/120%; Bulwark needs a shield; only one of the three at a time), Reinforced Armor
  (physical damage taken -4/7/10%), Spellwarded (magic damage taken -4/7/10%), Stoicism (damage-over-time
  taken -8/14/20%, pet too), Defensive Momentum (up to 5/7.5/10% damage reduction built by moving, lost
  after 2 s still) and Fortress (the same built by standing still, lost after 3 s moving; exclusive with
  Momentum). **At most 3 Defensive perks can be active.** Exclusive pairs became exclusive groups.
- **Gear attunement, stage 2 of 4: attuning.** Equipped items that count progress with every kill of a creature
  that would give experience (1 kill, elites 2, bosses 10; group members in range get it too) and are attuned
  for good at 50 kills (green), 100 (blue), 200 (epic) or 400 (legendary). Progress is kept per item
  (unequipping pauses it); chat shows 25/50/75% and "fully attuned" with the achievement sound. Players see
  their equipped items' progress with `.attune progress`.
- **11 Utility perks**, all with 3 ranks (levels 1 / 30 / 60, values in `mod_unbound.conf`):
  Longer Legs (run and mount speed +4/7/10%, swim +20/35/50%, fall damage -20/35/50%),
  Planting your legs (standing still 3 s: 0.5/0.75/1% health and 0.25/0.4/0.5% mana per second),
  Relentless Pursuit (+15/25/35% speed for 10 s after a kill), Vampirism (heal 3/4/5% of damage),
  Soul Drain (heal 6/8/10% of damage, pet too, direct healing taken -20%), Pets replenishment (pet healing
  taken +20/35/50%), Pets Roar (pet threat +100/200/300%), Silent Killer (threat -20/35/50%), Unbreakable
  Presence (threat +50/100/150%), Spell-Protected Steady Hands (pushback -30/50/70%, interrupt lockouts and
  silences -10/20/30%), Mana Leech (1/1.5/2% of damage as mana). Vampirism/Soul Drain and Silent
  Killer/Unbreakable Presence are exclusive pairs. **At most 3 Utility perks can be active** (like the 5
  Offensive). Speed, threat and Steady Hands use server-only passive spells 200210-200213 (no client patch).
- **Class perk target** in the power budget: each spec's class perk is worth about +25-35% to that spec's
  single-target damage (Retribution estimated at about +35-40%, to confirm on a training dummy).
- **Iron Will** (Utility perk, id 303): enemy crowd control on you and your pets (stun, fear, root, silence,
  sleep, polymorph, charm, disorient, sap) lasts 10% shorter at Rank 1 (level 1), 20% at Rank 2 (level 30)
  and 30% at Rank 3 (level 60). Stacks with racials, talents and diminishing returns. Values and rank levels
  are in `mod_unbound.conf` (`Unbound.Perks.IronWill.*`); GMs can test ranks with `.perk rank 303 <rank>`.
- **Perk ranks**, starting with Rapid Recovery: Rank 1 (6 seconds after combat, 4% health and mana per
  second) from level 1, Rank 2 (5 s, 6%) from level 30, Rank 3 (4 s, 8%) from level 60. Values and rank
  levels are in `mod_unbound.conf`. GMs set a rank with `.perk rank <perk id> <rank>` (saved per
  character; `.perk rank <perk id>` goes back to the level's rank). The perk window shows "2/3" badges and
  every rank in the tooltip.
- **Gear attunement, stage 1 of 4** (the collection and its stats): each character has a collection of
  attuned items that permanently give 20% of their fixed stats (applied like gear stats, shown on the
  character sheet), with diminishing returns (full value until 100% of a full set of gear, half until 200%,
  a quarter beyond). Counts: green or better weapons and armour, required level 50-60, fixed stats, from a
  released expansion. For now items are added with GM commands (`.attune add/remove/check/info/reset`);
  attuning by kills, the sacrifice altar and the tooltips/collection window follow.
- **Item expansion tags** (`tools/item_expansions.py`): 20,286 gear items tagged with the expansion they come
  from (8,612 classic, 4,870 TBC, 6,804 WotLK), so TBC and Wrath items can't count until those are released.
- **Perks tab on the character sheet.** A "Perks" tab after Character, Pet, Reputation, Skills and
  Currency, built from the game's own tab template. It opens the perk window beside the character sheet.
  With ElvUI's character skin on, ElvUI skins it like its other tabs.
- **Perk window: talent-tree style** (chosen look). Perk icons in a grid with action-button frames and
  talent-style rank badges (0/1, 1/1), a gold glow on active perks, greyed icons when a category is full,
  tooltips, right-click or double-click to toggle. Uses the game's own frame art (dialog border, title
  banner, red buttons). A Blizzard-style list version came first the same day and was replaced.
- **Paladin Retribution class perk:** Divine Storm hits up to 6 enemies, each also taking a Crusader
  Strike; Judgements spread to up to 4 more enemies with a free Exorcism on each; Exorcism scales with 30%
  of attack power and spell power; Righteous Vengeance explosions on up to 5 enemies; Holy damage +15%,
  Physical +10%.
- **All 25 Offensive perks**, at most 5 active at once: Cyclic Power, Critical Understanding, Defensive
  Sacrifice, Desperate Power, Block/Dodge/Parry/Haste Capping, Pet Encouragement, Rolling Momentum,
  Tunnel Vision, Collateral Damage, Giant Slayer, Merciless, Pressure, Chaos, Power Conversion, Healing
  Powah and the seven school Masteries.
- **Permanent perks** for every player: No Resurrection Sickness, No Daze, Rapid Recovery (8% health and
  mana per second, 4 seconds after combat).
- **Unbound Perks window** (`/perks`): categories, search, saved loadouts; the server keeps each
  character's perks. Defensive and Utility perks are listed but don't work yet.
- **World boss: Hrimthar the Frostbound**, a level 63 frost giant on Lake Kel'Theril in Winterspring,
  tuned for about 10 players, with six level 60 epics and a 3% Winterspring Frostsaber.
- **Heroic loot for The Deadmines:** one level 60 blue per boss from a tank/healer/damage pool, two from
