## 2026-10-17

### Changed
- **The Warden of Rare Wonders' prices, scaled to the Swift Spectral Tiger at 250 Sigils** (was 480; every item
  x 250/480, rounded to 5, at least 5): mounts 85-250 (were 160-480), pets 30-65 and the Scavenger Imp 85
  (were 60-120 and 160), tabards 15 (were 25), fun items 5-10 (were 5-20). Seven new prices: regenerate
  `patch-Z.MPQ`.

### Fixed
- **Health and mana from infusion survive a relog:** logging in capped your saved health and mana at the
  maximum without infusion (it is applied just after), so a character with infused Stamina or Intellect came
  back short and had to heal up. Now the saved health and mana are given back once infusion is applied.

## 2026-10-16

### Added
- **Lightbringer's Oath,** the Paladin's Holy class perk (perk 1003), built like the other two and exclusive with
  them (one Paladin class perk at a time). Its idea: a Holy paladin heals through a solo fight while the healing
  hurts the boss. Judgement, Exorcism and Holy Shock build **Holy Power** (the same buff and bar). The perk teaches
  **Light of Dawn** (30 yards: a Holy blast for 30% / 65% / 100% of 400 + 130% of spell power at 1 / 2 / 3 Holy
  Power, healing you for 30% of it) and **Atonement** (10 / 20 / 30 seconds in which 30% of all your healing,
  overhealing included, also hits the enemy you are fighting as Holy damage). Atonement picks that enemy itself:
  your target if it is an enemy, else what your friendly target is fighting (healing the tank hits the tank's
  enemy), else the last enemy you hit, else the nearest enemy attacking you or your group; it never hits an enemy
  held by crowd control that breaks on damage (Polymorph, Sap, ...), never pulls anything new, and in a group
  with other real players overhealing counts for a quarter (`Unbound.Perks.LightbringersOath.GroupOverheal`;
  bots don't count). Holy damage +10%. **3 ranks by level** (1, 30, 60); **Rank 2** unlocks the **oaths**:
  **the Dawn** (Holy Shock and Exorcism crits give 1 more Holy Power; Light of Dawn deals 30% more below 35%
  health) or **Mercy** (Atonement's damage heals you for 10% of it, Light of Dawn's self-heal doubled, your heals
  on yourself 10% stronger). **Rank 3** teaches **Naaru's Blessing** (2 minutes): a small Naaru stays 15 seconds,
  smites your enemy and heals you and your group within 10 yards (2% of maximum health each) every 2 seconds, gives Holy Power every third spell, and Atonement converts 50%
  meanwhile. Tuned to the new healer target in the Power budget (about 85-90% of a Retribution paladin's
  single-target damage, with more survival; to confirm on a training dummy). Needs the new `patch-Z.MPQ`.
- **Bulwark's Oath,** the Paladin's Protection class perk (perk 1002), built like Crusader's Oath and swapping
  with it (turning one on turns the other off). Hammer of the Righteous, Judgement and Avenger's Shield build
  **Holy Power** (the same buff and bar). The perk teaches two abilities that spend all of it: **Aegis Strike**
  (needs a shield, 6 seconds: a Holy strike for your block value + 50% of your attack power, times 45% / 98% /
  150% at 1 / 2 / 3 Holy Power) and **Word of Glory** (heals you for 8% / 16% / 25% of your maximum health). Holy
  damage +10%. **3 ranks by level** (1, 30, 60): Rank 1 is weaker (Aegis up to 100%, Word of Glory up to 15%,
  Holy +4%); **Rank 2** unlocks the **oaths** (button in the perk window): **the Bulwark** (Aegis Strike at 3
  Holy Power gives **Bastion**, 6 seconds of 10% less damage taken and 10% more block chance; the first Word of
  Glory during Bastion heals as if at 3 Holy Power; Word of Glory heals 30% more below 35% health) or
  **Retaliation** (blocks deal 30% of the blocked damage back as Holy damage, Consecration deals 20% more,
  Hammer of the Righteous has a 20% chance to reset Avenger's Shield). **Rank 3** teaches **Guardian of the
  Light** (3 minutes): a spectral paladin (the Ghost of Uther's look) fights your target for 15 seconds, you take
  30% less damage meanwhile, every third hit it lands gives Holy Power, and (**Ardent Light**) Holy Power
  abilities give 1 more and Holy Shield has 4 more charges. Tuned to the class perk budget, about +26-31%
  single-target damage at level 60 (to confirm on a training dummy). Settings `Unbound.Perks.BulwarksOath.*`.
  Needs the new `patch-Z.MPQ` (spells 200224-200228) and the UnboundPerks addon copied again.
- **Crusader's Oath** replaces the Paladin's Retribution class perk (same perk slot, id 1001, so it stays on for
  anyone who had Retribution on). Crusader Strike, Judgement and Hammer of Wrath build **Holy Power** (up to 3,
  a buff and a bar under the player frame; it fades 20 seconds after the last gain). The perk teaches two
  abilities that spend all of it: **Templar's Verdict** (a Holy strike for 60% / 130% / 200% weapon damage at
  1 / 2 / 3 Holy Power) and **Inquisition** (Holy damage +5% / 10% / 15% for 10 / 20 / 30 seconds). Physical
  damage +10%. It has **3 ranks by level** (1, 30, 60): Rank 1 is weaker (Verdict up to 120%, Inquisition up
  to 6%, Physical +4%); **Rank 2** (level 30) unlocks the **oaths**, chosen with a button in the perk window:
  **Vengeance** (Verdict crits deal 30% more, and 30% more on enemies below 35% health) or **the Crusade**
  (Divine Storm gives Holy Power and hits 6 enemies; at 3 Holy Power it spends them for 60% more damage, and
  each enemy it hits adds 5% to the next Verdict, up to 30%). **Rank 3** (level 60) teaches **Ashbringer's
  Call** (2 minutes): the Spirit of the Ashbringer fights your target for 15 seconds, and while it does
  (**Wings of Retribution**) Holy Power abilities give 1 more; every third hit it lands gives 1 Holy Power.
  Tuned to the class perk budget, about +28-32% single-target damage at level 60 (to confirm on a training
  dummy). Only one class perk can be active at a time. **The original Retribution perk is kept**: set
  `Unbound.Perks.Retribution.Classic = 1` and restart to switch back (the addon then shows the old perk).
  Needs the new `patch-Z.MPQ`. In the perk window its text is short: under the first line a row of icons
  (Holy Power, Templar's Verdict, Inquisition, Ashbringer's Call and the two oaths) explains each one on
  hover, with the numbers of your rank; the chosen oath is lit, the other dimmed, locked ones grey.
- **Client assets outside git:** icons and textures Unbound uses are listed in `data/client_assets.txt`; the
  files stay in a local `unbound-assets` folder beside the server (never committed). `tools/fetch_assets.py`
  fills it from a local WoW client's archives, and `tools/custom_dbc.py` packs them into `patch-Z.MPQ`.
- **Molten Core Firelords don't silence a solo player:** their Soul Burn (fire damage over time that also
  silences) fails while the raid has one player (`Unbound.SoloRaid.Firelord`). In groups it comes every 25 seconds
  instead of every 2-4 (it lasts 16, so the silence used to be up the whole fight).
- **Bounty Wayfinder** (beside both Bulletin Boards, Stormwind and Orgrimmar): teleports you close to the
  Bulletin Board's open-world quests, the 16 daily kill bounties and 12 weekly elite bounties. Your active ones
  are listed first, then "All bounty destinations". You arrive **90 yards up in the air** beside the camp, with
  **Wayfinder's Descent** (a slow fall that can't be cancelled and lasts until you land, no falling damage), so
  you see the camp from above and glide in. The drop point is at least 35 yards from any hostile creature (plus
  how far it wanders, and its patrol path) and 80 from either faction's guards. Camps in caves or tunnels
  (Hive'Regal, Mazthoril) teleport to a safe ground spot instead. Free; not in combat. Regenerate
  `patch-Z.MPQ` (spell 200204).
- **Scavenger Imp,** a third loot companion (160 Heroic Sigils, the Warden of Rare Wonders' Pets page): nothing
  is looted until you open a corpse yourself; then it loots up to 4 of the nearest other corpses within 27
  yards into your bags (`Unbound.ScavengerImp.ExtraCorpses` and `Unbound.ScavengerImp.Range`). The Loot Goblin
  and Arcane Loot Scanner keep their 55 yards and full auto-looting. Regenerate `patch-Z.MPQ`.
- **The Shadowflame Ward** (Kaelastrasz of the red dragonflight, Thorium Point in the Searing Gorge): a 12-quest
  chain, about as long as the Drakefire Amulet's, for an epic cloak (item level 66, 60 armor, 12 Stamina, 10 Fire
  and 10 Shadow Resistance) that protects from Shadowflame in Blackwing Lair like the Onyxia Scale Cloak, which
  can't be obtained here. Collect scales from all five dragonflights in any order: black (Burning Steppes, then
  the new elite Vexathrax there), red (Grim Batol, then Pyraxis), blue (Mazthoril, then Azurathys in Azshara's
  blue dragonkin camp), green (the three Nightmare camps) and bronze (Chronalis); then the **Scale of Onyxia**,
  now a drop from Onyxia for players on the quest, binds them into the ward. Vexathrax, Pyraxis and Azurathys
  use the models and fights of the rares Teremus the Devourer, Narillasanz and Azurous, at level 60 with a
  10-minute respawn. Regenerate `patch-Z.MPQ` (10 new items).
- **Warden of Rare Wonders** (`.npc add 190200`, Tyrande Whisperwind's look): cosmetics for Heroic Sigils on four
  tabs. Mounts 40-120 (Riding Turtle, Spectral Tiger, Swift Zhevra, Celestial Steed, The Horseman's Reins, Magic
  Rooster, Swift Spectral Tiger), pets 15-30 (20, from Onyxian Whelpling to Lil' K.T.), tabards 25 (the 8 card-game
  tabards), fun items 5-20 (Noggenfogger Elixir to Orb of Deception). Only items a level 60 character can use on a
  classic-only server. Six new Sigil prices (5, 40, 75, 90, 100, 120): regenerate `patch-Z.MPQ`.
- **Warden of Rare Wonders with Ysera's look** (`.npc add 190205`): the same vendor in Ysera's night elf form
  (display 28640, from Ulduar), to place instead of or beside the Tyrande one.

### Changed
- **Crusader's Oath in the perk window:** the Oath of Vengeance and Oath of the Crusade icons have their own row
  under the "Your oath" heading (the chosen one lit, the other dimmed) instead of a line naming the chosen oath;
  the Physical damage text is under them. Copy the UnboundPerks addon and `/reload`.
- **Infusion window in pages:** the Infused and In progress tabs show 60 items a page (10 x 6) with the Spellbook's

  UnboundAttunement addon and `/reload`.
- **Perk window, ranked perks:** the list of every rank under a perk's text is now one gold line ("Rank 2 / 3  -  next
  at level 60"); hovering it lists every rank and what it gives, the current one green.
- **Infusion progress on item tooltips is a bar** like the experience bar (purple, ten segments, the percentage
  on it) instead of the "Infusion: % (x/x kills)" line. Copy the UnboundAttunement addon and `/reload`.
- **The Warden of Rare Wonders' mounts and pets cost four times as many Heroic Sigils:** mounts 160-480 (were
  40-120), pets 60-120 (were 15-30), the Scavenger Imp 160 (was 40). Tabards and fun items are unchanged.
  Regenerate `patch-Z.MPQ` (seven new prices).


### Fixed
- **Class perk abilities listed twice in the Spellbook** (Templar's Verdict, Inquisition, Ashbringer's Call, and
  Bulwark's Oath's the same way): the core sends the "learned" packet twice for a temporary spell taught with
  `learnSpell`. A relog clears copies already shown.
- **Class perk abilities vanished from the action bar on relog:** the core loads action buttons before the perks
  teach their abilities, and drops buttons for spells the character doesn't know yet. The abilities are now saved
  with the character like trained spells (and still unlearned when the perk is turned off), which also fixes the
  Spellbook doubles. Put them back on your bars once; they stay after that.
- **Startup warnings for the Blackened Defias Garb set:** its four pieces now have `item_set_names` rows
  (`tools/heroic_loot.py`), so the server no longer reports them missing.
- **patch-Z no longer hides other client patches' item displays:** `tools/custom_dbc.py` adds Unbound's rows to the
  client's own copy of a DBC when another patch changed it (e.g. an HD item pack's `ItemDisplayInfo.dbc`, whose
  model and geoset changes patch-Z used to undo), instead of always starting from the stock copy. Regenerate
  `patch-Z.MPQ` with the game closed.
- **Locale patches in the asset load order:** `tools/fetch_assets.py` ranks `Data\enUS\patch-enUS-3.MPQ` and
  the like right after their unlocalized patch, as the game does (they used to rank as base archives).

## 2026-10-15

### Added
- **Heroic Sigils drop, and the Heroic Quartermaster (Stage B).** Every Heroic boss drops 1 Heroic Sigil, the
  final boss of each dungeon and wing 2 (all 189 Heroic bosses; everyone in the group loots their own). Stormwind
  Stockades and Ragefire Chasm's Heroic bosses now have their own loot tables (their normal loot plus the Sigils).
  The weekly Heroic dungeon quests on the Bulletin Board give 10 Sigils plus 75 gold (was 100 gold). The **Heroic
  Quartermaster** (`.npc add 190104`) sells all 450 Heroic items for Sigils from a menu of five pages: rings,
  necks, cloaks, wrists and belts 10; head, shoulders, chest 15; hands, legs, feet 15; one-handers, shields and
  off-hands 20, two-handers and ranged 25; final boss treasures 30.
- **Heroic Sigil, the Heroic currency (Stage A).** Item 912000 with a gold lion's head icon, in the Currency tab
  under a new "Unbound" header; soulbound, takes no bag space, multi-drop (every group member gets their own from
  loot). **One shared amount per account:** every character of an account sees and spends the same Sigils (set at
  login, saved at every save and at logout). Characters logged in as bots and GM accounts keep their own amounts
  (`Unbound.AccountCurrency.GMAccounts` turns sharing on for GM accounts, for testing). Not dropping anywhere yet:
  boss drops and the Heroic Quartermaster are Stage B.
- **Focused Mind** (Utility perk, id 316, Rogues and Druids only): energy regenerates +8 / 14 / 20% faster at
  levels 1 / 30 / 60 and 26 / 30% at Ranks 4-5 (GM-only for now). A server-only aura like the Vitality talent
  (spell 200217, energy regeneration %), so the energy bar fills faster in the client too.
- **Endless Rage** (Utility perk, id 315, Warriors and Druids only): more rage from damage you deal and take,
  +8 / 14 / 20% at levels 1 / 30 / 60 and 26 / 30% at Ranks 4-5 (GM-only for now). Rage from damage dealt is a
  server-only aura like the Endless Rage talent (spell 200216, stacks with it); rage from damage taken is added
  with the core's own formula. Class-limited perks can now be for several classes (a class mask).

### Changed
- **The Heroic Quartermaster's pages are sorted:** by slot (head to feet, then rings, trinkets and weapons), then
  armor type (cloth, leather, mail, plate) or weapon type, then alphabetically.
- **Infusion is a side tab on the character sheet too,** under the Perks side tab, instead of a micro menu button
  (whose icon ElvUI's micro bar sometimes hid). The two side tabs act like tabs: opening one window closes the other.
- **Perk Ranks 4 and 5 stay GM-only** by design (`.perk rank`); players don't earn them, so the roadmap no longer
  plans a way to earn or buy them. The Power budget no longer counts them (about x1.3): a fully infused player is about 4.6x
  (5.8-6.3x with the class perk), so Mythic gear now also carries the realistic goal, Naxxramas Mythic +1.
- **Mythic gear infuses at 20% + 3% per Mythic level** (planned; was 2%): a +10 item infuses at 50%, which keeps the
  stretch goal, Naxxramas Mythic +10, in reach without perk Ranks 4-5.
- **The Perks tab is a side tab on the character sheet:** a square icon tab on its right edge (the Spellbook's side
  tab template), instead of a sixth bottom tab, which didn't fit once the Currency tab appeared. Lit while the perk
  window is open; ElvUI's side tab look with ElvUI's character skin.
- **Daily kill bounties with one target got a second:** Mossflayer Trolls (10 Shadowhunter + 8 Cannibal), Ice Thistle
  Yetis (10 Yeti + 8 Patriarch) and The Blackrock Clan (10 Slayer + 8 Warlock), instead of 12 of one type, like the
  other bounties.
- **Raid-tuned elite bounty camps at normal elite strength** (`unbound_elite_bounty_scaling.sql`): the Emerald portal
  guardians (Verdantine, Jademir, Emeraldon), the Tainted Scar demons and the Isle of Dread Chimaerok had 7-25x
  health (23,000-81,000; the open world isn't scaled by autobalance); now 3x like the other bounty camps (7,500-9,700).
  Lord Lakmaeran 6x (about 19,400, was 324,000). This changes them for everyone in the open world.
- **Ossirian solo:** alone in Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, his Curse of Tongues fails and his Enveloping Winds comes at most
